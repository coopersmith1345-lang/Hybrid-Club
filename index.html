The Club

Every discipline.
One community.

Most clubs make you pick a lane. Lifters lift, runners run, and the people training across all of it end up training alone. Hybrid Club exists to change that.

We're a community in Sydney's Hills District for people who train seriously around full-time work and study. It starts with a weekly run. Strength, ride and swim sessions come next. The standard is showing up, not keeping up.

I'm from the Hills area in Sydney. I first found my passion in the gym, and over the years it's turned into hybrid and triathlon training. I want to get better at every discipline: swimming, cycling and running. The main reason we created this club is to bring young, like-minded people together, not just to train but to build friendships. I know from experience how isolating it is to only ever train by yourself.

I'm from the Hills in Sydney. I've found my love of fitness over the years, whether that's running, lifting weights, attempting a crazy Pilates move or cooking a high-protein meal. Fitness has changed the way I look at life and how I can keep improving. As a nurse I know first-hand how important a healthy life is, from two litres of water a day to eight hours of sleep.
